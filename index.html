ChurnSense

Customer Churn Predictor — logistic regression, trained on 800 synthetic telecom customers. All prediction happens in your browser; no data leaves this page.

Enter the customer details and press Predict churn risk.

Model: 5-fold cross-validated accuracy = 73.5%, AUC = 0.82 (guessing the majority class: 50.6% — about 1.45× better). Risk above 50% is flagged as likely to churn. Trained on synthetic data for learning purposes; real churn also depends on service quality, offers and competitors.
